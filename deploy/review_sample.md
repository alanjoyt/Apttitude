### 1. [Quantitative Aptitude] ID #1439 - Hexaware (Ratio & Proportion)
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Source Tag:** `Hexaware Practice Pattern`

**Question:**
> The ratio of boys to girls in a school of 720 students is 7 : 5. How many more girls must be admitted to make the ratio 1 : 1?

**Options:**
- **A)** 90
- **B)** 100
- **C)** 120
- **D)** 140

**Stored Correct Answer:** `Option C`

**Stored Explanation:**
Total parts = 12. 1 part = 720/12 = 60. Boys = 7*60 = 420. Girls = 5*60 = 300. Difference to make 1:1 = 420 - 300 = 120 girls.
